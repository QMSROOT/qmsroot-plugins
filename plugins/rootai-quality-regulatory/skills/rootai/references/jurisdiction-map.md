# Jurisdiction map

Where to look for official sources, by market. This file names regulators, instruments and sites as starting points only. **Every entry is unverified until checked**: confirm the instrument's current name, number and version on the official site before stating anything about it, and apply the verification labels in source-policy.md.

Depth: **Deep** = ROOTAi works the question in detail. **Standard** = works the question, more readily flags specialist review. **Outline** = gives an outline, verifies what it can, and says that a local regulatory expert is needed.

| Framework / market | Regulator or owner | Instruments (names only) | Official sites to search and fetch | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ISO 13485, ISO 9001, ISO 14971 | ISO | The standards themselves (paywalled) | The user's own licensed copy; iso.org for edition status only | Deep, paraphrase only |
| Product standards: IEC 60601-1 (electrical medical equipment safety), IEC 62304 (medical device software lifecycle), ISO 10993 series (biological evaluation) | IEC; ISO | The standards themselves and their collateral, particular or part standards (paywalled). Which editions a market recognises is set by that market | The user's own licensed copy; iec.ch and iso.org for edition status only; each market's official list of recognised, harmonised or designated standards | Standard, paraphrase only (specialist skill planned) |

Notes:
- International guidance (for example IMDRF documents on imdrf.org) is *Official guidance*: useful for principles, not binding by itself.
- For Outline markets: official texts may be available only in the local language; an English summary or translation is at most *Official source, currency not confirmed*. Say that requirements such as local representation, registration and language rules need confirmation by a local expert.
- Do not infer one market's requirements from another's.
- Product standards: the edition and amendments that apply depend on the market and on what the user's test house, Notified Body or regulator accepts. Ask which edition the user is working to; never assume it. Test results, software safety classification and biological evaluation conclusions are the user's and their experts' to establish.
